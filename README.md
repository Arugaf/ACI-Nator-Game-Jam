## A Curiosity Shop

You run a souvenir shop in a bustling bazaar. Crowds of tourists crave authentic Indonesian exotica, but ordinary trinkets won't deter them: each souvenir must be awakened by a sacred ablution in one of the four patron bowls.

## How to please a guest?

Choose a bowl: bless the souvenir with the correct patron—Barong, Dewi Sri, Semar, or Garuda.

Be bold in your offerings: tourists don't really know what they want until they see it with their own eyes. Try and test your guesses!

Heed the signs: if you make a mistake with your offering, the spirit will punish you personally, but the tourist will hint with emotion which category their soul belongs to.

Wrath of the Four Forces:
Don't confuse the bowls! Barong will inflate you to the size of a hulking giant, Semar will perform a shadow play and extinguish the lights, Garuda will whisk you away to the far corner of the shop, and the insulted Devi Sri will make a tourist lose his temper twice as fast.

Purification through the Gate:
Have you grabbed the wrong item or corrupted it with someone else's aura? Throw the damaged rarity into the sacred gates of Chandi Bentar—the portal will absorb the error and restore peace to the shop.
